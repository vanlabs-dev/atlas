# Bittensor Ground Truth

Verified facts. Every article MUST be consistent with these. If unsure about a mechanic, state what you know and flag the uncertainty. Do NOT guess.

## Emissions and Tokens

- Emissions happen in two stages (per the official docs).
  - Stage 1, every block (~12s): the protocol injects BOTH TAO and alpha into the subnet's AMM pool. The TAO amount is sized by the subnet's gated price-based emission share (EMA price x (1 - miner_burn), normalized, then passed through the emission gate; see the emission model section); alpha is injected to keep the pool price stable, with the alpha injection capped by the subnet's root proportion (excess TAO routes to chain buys, see the emission section).
  - Stage 2, end of each tempo (360 blocks, ~72 minutes): the alpha accumulated over the tempo is distributed to participants through the subnet's epoch consensus: Yuma Consensus by default, or Null consensus on a subnet whose owner selected it (see the epoch consensus section).
- The distribution split is set PER SUBNET by its emission weight vector `[burn, recycle, owner, validator, miner]` (see the emission weights section). Subnets start at 18% owner / 41% miners / 41% validators and their stakers, and the subnet owner (or root) can change it within chain-enforced bounds. Never state a given subnet's split without a chain read of `SubnetEmissionWeights`. What an individual staker nets out also varies with the validator's take (default 18%).
- Participants on a subnet receive alpha; the injected TAO backs the pool reserve. Stakers can keep alpha or swap it to TAO. Do not describe emissions as a single token: both TAO and alpha are injected.
- TAO is the root network token. Alpha tokens are subnet-specific tokens.
- When you stake TAO on a subnet, it is converted to alpha via the subnet's AMM (automated market maker) liquidity pool: the TAO enters the pool's TAO reserve and the pool returns the equivalent alpha at the current rate.
- The alpha price (in TAO) is determined by the AMM pool ratio, not set manually.

## Subnets

- The network is capped at 128 occupied subnet slots (the subnet limit). Netuid 0 is the root subnet (Subnet Zero): a special subnet, and the only one without its own alpha token. (A raise to 256 slots was drafted for the spec 440 release but dropped before it shipped; the cap remains 128. Do not report 256 as live.)
- Subnet deregistration is automatic (live since September 2025). When a new subnet registers and all 128 slots are full, the protocol deregisters the subnet with the LOWEST EMA price among non-immune subnets (ties broken by earliest registration). This happens at most once every ~2 days (14,400 blocks).
- New subnets have a network immunity period during which they cannot be deregistered: 60 days (432,000 blocks, `NetworkImmunityPeriod`, explicit at finalized block 9,348,505 on 2026-10-24). Root lowered it from 120 days on 2026-10-14 with no runtime upgrade, so subnets registered 60 to 120 days before that date lost their immunity at once. After its immunity a subnet is judged like any other and can be pruned when a new subnet registers.
- When a subnet is deregistered, its AMM pools are dissolved: all alpha is converted to TAO and returned proportionally to alpha holders. The freed netuid can be taken by a new registration, so a netuid can change hands.
- A dissolved subnet stays visible in the chain's reporting runtime APIs (metagraph, dynamic info, neuron, subnet-state and stake-info calls) until its background cleanup finishes. The chain tracks this in `SubnetState` (`Registered`, `Started`, `PendingDissolution`, `Dissolving`), separately from `NetworksAdded`, which still decides whether a call can target the subnet. During cleanup a hotkey's stake reports as zero once that hotkey has been paid out, and no stake is reported as available to unstake. A subnet listed by those APIs is therefore not necessarily live. Atlas does not read `SubnetState`.
- Each subnet has a tempo: a 360-block period (~72 minutes at 12s/block) over which the subnet's epoch consensus (Yuma by default, or Null) calculates emissions.
- Creating a subnet costs a dynamic "burn cost" in TAO (it doubles at each new registration, then falls by one last-lock amount per lock reduction interval: it halves in one interval and reaches the 1 TAO floor in two. The interval is 8 days (57,600 blocks, `NetworkLockReductionInterval`, explicit at finalized block 9,348,505); root halved it from 16 days on 2026-10-14. The 2-day minimum gap between registrations, `NetworkRateLimit` 14,400 blocks, did not change). This TAO is recycled, not destroyed. Spec 453 moved pricing and the rate-limit update to queue time for deferred registrations, and reserves the hotkey for the paying coldkey. Do not describe burn cost as charged only at the moment the slot is occupied.
- A registration must pay the full quoted price and leave the coldkey above the existential deposit; a short payment fails and creates no subnet. A queued (deferred) registration PREPAYS the price into its own escrow account (`NetworkRegistrationEscrow`, one entry per queue item) instead of locking the registrant's balance, so later holds or transfers on the coldkey cannot leave it unfunded. Settlement pays the subnet from that escrow. If settlement fails for any reason other than the subnet limit, the escrow is refunded to the coldkey, the entry leaves the queue, and `NetworkRegistrationCancelled` is emitted; no subnet is created. The queue settles at most one entry per idle block. A new subnet's pool TAO is exactly the TAO paid: the chain does not top it up to the minimum lock cost.

## Miners and Validators

- Miners produce work (inference, training, data, compute, etc. depending on the subnet).
- Validators evaluate miner output and set weights that determine emission distribution.
- Validators stake on subnets to participate. Their stake-weighted votes determine miner rewards.
- Anyone can register a hotkey on a subnet to mine. Validating is permissioned by stake: on a Yuma subnet only the top 64 hotkeys by stake/emissions (subject to a minimum stake) receive validator permits by default. On a Null-consensus subnet exactly ONE hotkey holds a permit (see the epoch consensus section).
- Two registration routes, each switched per subnet by its owner (or root):
  - BURN registration (`burned_register`; the legacy `register` and `register_limit` calls also pay by burn despite their names) pays the subnet's burn price in TAO. `sudo_set_network_registration_allowed` toggles it.
  - PROOF-OF-WORK registration (`pow_register`, call index 152) pays no TAO burn, buys no collateral and pays no transaction fee. It works only where the owner turned it on (`NetworkPowRegistrationAllowed`, default off; `sudo_set_network_pow_registration_allowed`). The proof binds the subnet, a work block at most 5 blocks old, the hotkey and the signing coldkey, and transaction validation checks it before the pool admits the call. One hotkey cannot reuse an older work block. PoW has its own difficulty price: a PoW registration raises difficulty, not the burn price, and both prices decay with the subnet's shared half-life.
  - The subnet owner or root sets the PoW difficulty bounds and price: `min_difficulty` (`sudo_set_min_difficulty`, call index 4), `difficulty` (`sudo_set_difficulty`, call index 24) and `max_difficulty`. Owner changes run inside the admin window, under a separate owner rate limit per parameter, and can go through an `Owner` proxy, which also admits the PoW toggle. Root can override the owner cooldown. On root (netuid 0) only root can set difficulty. A `min_difficulty` of `u64::MAX` pins difficulty at maximum and in effect shuts PoW off, so an owner reopens PoW by lowering `min_difficulty` and then setting `difficulty` (the two can go in one `Utility.batch_all`). Do not describe these two difficulty settings as root-only.
  - An owner cannot switch both routes off at once. Root (netuid 0) is burn-only. Do not describe PoW registration as removed or as available on every subnet; whether a given subnet accepts it needs a chain read.

## Epoch consensus: Yuma or Null

- Each non-root subnet runs one epoch reward algorithm, stored per netuid in `SubnetEpochConsensus`: **Yuma** (the default for existing and new subnets) or **Null**. The subnet owner or root selects it with `AdminUtils::sudo_set_epoch_consensus` (call index 111), inside the admin window and under the owner hyperparameter rate limit. The subnet hyperparameters report it as `epoch_consensus` (0 = Yuma, 1 = Null). The tempo, the subnet's emission weight vector and the per-block emission are the same in both modes. Atlas does not read `SubnetEpochConsensus`: never state which subnets run Null without a chain read.
- NULL CONSENSUS, per epoch:
  - The ONE hotkey with the largest stake (alpha plus TAO-weighted root stake, including parent/child delegation; ties go to the lowest UID) holds the only validator permit. The owner's UID gets no automatic permit.
  - Miner incentive follows that validator's weight row alone, in its raw ratios. There is no stake-weighted median, no clipping and no bonds. If the row is empty, every registered UID shares the miner budget equally.
  - Validator dividends go to EVERY UID with positive stake, in proportion to that stake, whether or not it holds the permit or is active. A subnet with no staked UID pays the whole local budget to miners, equally.
  - Only the permit holder can set, commit or reveal weights. Other hotkeys, including the owner's, are rejected (`NeuronNoValidatorPermit`).
  - `MaxAllowedValidators` is pinned to 1. The UID cap is 2,500 shared across all mechanisms (Yuma keeps its default cap). Timelocked commits share a 64 KiB / 64-entry queue per subnet epoch, and a higher-stake validator's commit can displace lower-priority ones.
  - Scheduling: a block in which a Null subnet's epoch is due runs only one epoch, and the due Null subnet takes that slot first. Other subnets due in that block defer to a later block. A subnet earlier in registration order cannot hold the slot and starve the Null epoch. Blocks with no due Null subnet keep the configured per-block epoch cap.
- Switching keeps the subnet's history. Bonds freeze while Null runs and Yuma resumes from the last Yuma epoch's bonds. Leaving Null restores the saved Yuma validator limit. A switch is refused while timelocked weight commits are pending, or while the subnet has more UIDs than the target mode allows. A Null subnet shrinks only through explicit pruning: `sudo_trim_null_uids_batch` (call index 112) removes at most 64 UIDs per call toward a fixed target.
- Do not describe every subnet as running Yuma, and do not read a single permit, missing bonds or one weight-setting validator on a Null subnet as a fault.

## dTAO and the Emission Model

- Dynamic TAO (dTAO) gave each subnet its own alpha token and AMM liquidity pool. (The precise launch date is not stated on the canonical docs pages reached; treat "early 2025" as approximate until confirmed.)
- The emission model has changed several times. dTAO originally allocated emissions by price. In November 2025 it switched to flow-based ("Taoflow"). In June 2026 it REVERTED to a price-based model (subtensor v3.4.6-421, PR #2781), initially weighted by root_prop. Two further changes landed in July 2026: root_prop was REMOVED from the share (spec 432, live on Finney 2026-07-16), and an EMISSION GATE was added on top (spec 440, live on Finney 2026-07-27). A third landed in August 2026: the gate's BAR SELECTION RULE changed from a q-mass quantile to a RANK PIN (spec 441, PR #3014, live on Finney 2026-08-03). The GATED price-based model with a rank-pinned bar is the CURRENT model. (Live Finney spec 484, verified 2026-10-24.)
- Finney `spec_version` is **484** (verified 2026-10-24, Finney RPC, finalized block 9348505). This corpus states live mechanics, not the upgrade train. Each subnet's owner can set how its alpha emission divides between owner, validators and miners, and root stakers are paid their baseline share off the top of the validator slot. Subnets choose Yuma or Null epoch consensus, and owners can enable fee-free PoW registration and set its difficulty. New subnets register with the pool-side emission switch on. Validator-directed basket trading is on. A coldkey can refuse staking-list growth caused by other accounts, and a single-subnet hotkey swap leaves a delegator's stake on the old hotkey when it cannot follow without growing that list. A coldkey swap can merge into an already-used coldkey, and a cleartext announcement reserves its destination. A validator hotkey can delegate its operations to a narrow `Validate` or `Weights` proxy. Unused declared fees are refunded to alpha payers in TAO. A validator can rebalance its fund with one-leg `swap_basket` or with multi-leg `swap_basket_many`. A dissolved subnet stays in the chain's reporting APIs until its cleanup finishes. Do not describe the cash-first claim path as live: it was written for 468 and dropped before that spec shipped.
- CURRENT model (gated price-based, since 2026-07-27, spec 440; rank-pinned bar since 2026-08-03, spec 441): a subnet's demand share is its EMA price weighted by (1 - miner_burn) and normalized across emit-enabled subnets; that share is then passed through the emission gate and renormalized:
  `demand_share_i = (price_i * (1 - miner_burn_i)) / sum_j(price_j * (1 - miner_burn_j))`
  `emission_share_i = gate(demand_share_i) * demand_share_i / sum_j(gate(demand_share_j) * demand_share_j)`
  - **price** is the subnet's EMA (moving) price in TAO (the on-chain SubnetMovingPrice), NOT the spot/active price.
  - **the emission gate** (spec 440) is a Hill function: gate(s) = s^h / (s^h + theta^h). theta ("the bar", on-chain EmissionGateBar) is recomputed every 360 blocks (~72 min). The gate passes exactly 1/2 at the bar, ~1 well above it, ~0 well below it. Net effect: below-bar (low-demand) subnets' emission collapses toward zero and is redistributed upward, so above-bar subnets receive MORE than their raw demand share. A zero bar disables the gate, and safety fallbacks restore the ungated shares if fixed-point underflow would otherwise zero every subnet.
  - **how the bar is selected** has TWO modes, chosen by the on-chain EmissionBarRank (N). Both operate only over POSITIVE demand shares, so registering empty subnets does not move the bar in either mode.
    - RANK MODE (N > 0) is the CURRENT mode (spec 441, live 2026-08-03): theta is pinned to the Nth-largest positive demand share, so exactly the top N subnets sit at or above the gate midpoint however the distribution shifts. If fewer than N subnets have positive demand, theta falls to the smallest positive share and the gate stops biting (everyone passes). The CODE default at v443 is N = 32 (pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs, DefaultEmissionBarRank), chosen because q = 0.75 was crossing at about rank 28, so the change was deliberately tuned NOT to shift the emission curve. NOTE: the v441 RELEASE NOTES state the default is 64 and are WRONG; the code is authoritative.
    - Q-MASS MODE (N = 0) is the spec-440 rule and is now only a FALLBACK: sort demand shares descending and accumulate until the running total crosses q; the share at the crossing is the bar, so subnets above it collectively carry q of total demand.
    - Consequence: while rank mode is active, EmissionBarQuantile is INERT. Do not describe the bar as a q-mass quantile, and do not quote a live q as if it set the bar. Live Finney 2026-10-24 (spec 484, finalized block 9348505): EmissionBarRank unset (default 32, rank mode ACTIVE), EmissionBarQuantile explicit at 0.75 and unused, EmissionGateExponent unset (default 3).
    - A rank-pinned bar IS a demand share, so it moves as the distribution moves. A subnet can change side without its own demand changing at all - the bar can descend onto it or rise past it. When reporting a crossing, distinguish "this subnet's share moved" from "the bar moved"; they are different facts. Worked example: when spec 441 reset the bar on 2026-08-03, theta fell about 14.5% in a single poll and four subnets (49, 67, 79, 81) crossed above in one pass while their shares stood still.
  - **the bar parameters are all root-sudo-settable** (N as u16, q in (0,1), h in [1,8]) and changes happen via root/multisig paths that leave no obvious extrinsic trail. Never state a current N, q, or h without a live chain read; the code defaults are not the live values. This is not hypothetical - live q was already 0.75 against a 0.61 code default when first read on 2026-07-28.
  - **root_prop is NO LONGER in the share formula** (removed in spec 432, live 2026-07-16). root_prop = (T_root * w) / (T_root * w + alpha_issuance), where T_root is network-wide root TAO stake, w is the network tao weight, and alpha_issuance is the subnet's own alpha issuance. It still exists on-chain and still (a) caps the per-block alpha injection (root_prop x alpha_emission) and (b) scaled by the default validator share, sets root's baseline cut of the subnet's emission (see the emission weights section), but it does NOT weight emission share anymore. Do not include it in the current share formula; equally, do not deny it was ever there. It weighted the share only from June 2026 (spec 421) to 2026-07-16 (spec 432).
  - **miner_burn** is the proportion (0..1) of a subnet's miner (incentive) emission in a tempo that is withheld from miners because it goes to an owner/immune hotkey, counted whether it is recycled OR burned. The (1 - miner_burn) term cuts the subnet's chain emission share in lock step: a subnet that routes all miner rewards to the owner/burn key drives its share toward zero; one that stops recovers full share the next tempo (recomputed fresh each tempo, no memory). Validators operate this lever via the weights they set; the triumvirate oversight tooling is the backstop for fake-mining subnets where validators are inactive. The announcement frames it as a boolean (burn all or nothing), but on-chain it is a continuous proportion. (Verified still present in the v440 code.)
  - **the pool-side emission switch** (`SubnetEmissionEnabled`) is a SEPARATE mechanism from the emission-gate bar. See the dedicated section below. The bar is a continuous Hill function of demand share; this switch is a binary root-settable per-netuid bool. The chain selects the bar over the share distribution first and applies the switch afterwards, zeroing and redistributing the TAO side only. A subnet with the switch off still sits in the distribution the bar is drawn from.

## Pool-side emission switch (SubnetEmissionEnabled)

- A root-settable per-netuid boolean in `SubtensorModule`. Subnet registration writes it on. After that only `AdminUtils::sudo_set_subnet_emission_enabled` (call index 94, `ensure_root(origin)`) changes it. The subnet owner cannot flip it.
- It is NOT the emission-gate bar. The bar is a continuous function of demand share. This switch is binary, and on an existing subnet only a root-origin call changes it.
- When the switch is off, the subnet keeps distributing alpha to its participants (`alpha_out` continues). Its TAO-side share is zeroed and renormalised across the subnets whose switch is on (`pallets/subtensor/src/coinbase/subnet_emissions.rs`). A subnet with substantial demand share can therefore earn no TAO at all.
- New subnets register with the switch ON, including a reused netuid. No root call is needed: once the owner's `start_call` activates the subnet, it can earn its TAO share. Registration does not touch existing subnets, so a subnet root has switched off stays off. Root can still turn any subnet's switch off. The storage default for a netuid with no key is on.
- Live Finney 2026-10-24 (spec 484, finalized block 9348505): 128 keys; 126 on; **82 and 103 off**. SN1 is on. Only a root call changes the switch on an existing subnet, so read it before naming an off subnet.
- Do not answer "why does this subnet earn no TAO?" with the emission-gate bar alone. Check the switch. Positive demand share does not imply the subnet earns TAO.
- HISTORICAL eras (do NOT describe any of these as current):
  - Original dTAO price-based (February 2025 to November 2025).
  - Taoflow (November 2025 to June 2026): emission share was determined by NET TAO FLOWS (staking minus unstaking), smoothed with an EMA. The flow code path is dead in the runtime; the chain still records net flows, but they no longer drive emission, so a "net flow" reading is a demand signal only.
  - Price-based WITH root_prop weighting (June 2026, spec 421, to 2026-07-16, spec 432): share proportional to root_prop x EMA price x (1 - miner_burn).
  - Ungated price-based (2026-07-16, spec 432, to 2026-07-27, spec 440): share proportional to EMA price x (1 - miner_burn), no gate.
  - Gated with a Q-MASS bar (2026-07-27, spec 440, to 2026-08-03, spec 441): the gate as now, but theta was the q-mass crossing share rather than a rank pin. Spec 441 ran a one-shot migration (`migrate_reset_emission_gate_bar`) clearing the stale quantile bar so rank 32 applied at upgrade.
- TAO holders express preference by staking into specific subnets (buying alpha), which lifts the subnet's price and therefore its emission share. This is "voting with your TAO." Since the emission gate (spec 440), demand that stays below the bar earns almost nothing: crossing the bar is now the meaningful threshold for emission.

## Emissions and Halving

- Daily TAO emission is currently ~3,600 TAO (0.5 TAO per block). The first halving cut block rewards by 50% (from 1.0 to 0.5 TAO/block, i.e. ~7,200 to ~3,600 TAO/day).
- Halvings are SUPPLY-BASED, not calendar-based: a halving triggers automatically when total TAO issuance reaches preset thresholds, so timing depends on issuance, not a fixed date. (The first halving landed around December 2025; treat the date as approximate, the trigger is issuance.)
- TAO has a hard cap of 21 million tokens (the same 21M cap applies to each subnet's alpha token). Circulating supply changes over time; do not state a fixed circulating figure.
- Every block (~12 seconds) TAO is injected into subnet pools based on each subnet's gated price-based emission share (EMA price x (1 - miner_burn), normalized, then passed through the emission gate). At the end of each tempo (360 blocks, ~72 minutes), the tempo's accumulated alpha is distributed through the subnet's epoch consensus (Yuma by default, or Null).
- The alpha distribution split is per subnet (the emission weight vector below). Subnets start at 18% owner / 41% miners / 41% validators and their stakers. Individual staker payouts also vary with the validator's take (default 18%).

## Emission weights (per-subnet split)

- Every non-root subnet stores a five-slot emission weight vector `[burn, recycle, owner, validator, miner]` in `SubnetEmissionWeights`. Each slot is a `u64` share of `u64::MAX`, and the five slots sum to exactly one. Each block, `alpha_out x owner` accrues to the owner, `alpha_out x validator` to the validator bucket, and `alpha_out x miner` to miner incentive, which is split across the subnet's mechanisms. On a Yuma subnet the epoch pays miners the accumulated miner budget in full, divided in proportion to incentive score, with rounding remainders given out in UID order. A subnet's validator or root share therefore does not change what its miners receive. New subnets are seeded with the default vector, `[0, 0, 0.18, 0.41, 0.41]` on mainnet. At the upgrade every existing subnet was seeded from its old owner-cut state, so no subnet's payout changed until its owner acted. A subnet that had the owner cut disabled was seeded at owner 0, validator 0.5, miner 0.5.
- The subnet owner or root sets it with `AdminUtils::sudo_set_subnet_emission_weights` (call index 114). Updates are sparse: name the slots to change, and the merged vector must still sum to one. Rules: burn = 0 and recycle = 0 (reserved, a nonzero value is rejected); owner 0 to 33% (a subnet created through a lease, `SubnetCreatedViaLease`, cannot go below 18%); validator at most 50%; miner at least 33%. The call runs inside the admin window. The owner may change the vector once per 50,400 blocks (about a week). The limit is keyed per subnet, so a new owner inherits it. A subnet's first change is never rate-limited, root is exempt, and dissolving a subnet clears the limit. Each change emits `SubnetEmissionWeightsSet` with the full vector. The EVM subnet precompile exposes `setSubnetEmissionWeights` and `getSubnetEmissionWeights`. The runtime API `get_subnet_emission_weights` returns the vector plus `root_baseline_share` and `root_share`.
- An owner slot of 0 means no owner cut. The legacy owner-cut controls are frozen: `sudo_set_subnet_owner_cut` (call 28), `sudo_set_owner_cut_enabled` (call 92) and the precompile `setOwnerCutEnabled` always fail with `Deprecated`. `SubnetOwnerCut` and `OwnerCutEnabled` remain in storage, but no emission path reads them. Do not compute a subnet's owner or miner share from them.
- ROOT OFF THE TOP: root TAO stakers are paid out of the validator slot first. Each block the chain computes root's baseline share `r = V0 x x / (x + A)`, with `V0` the default validator share (0.41 on mainnet, one global constant), `x = root_tao x TaoWeight` (the global TaoWeight) and `A` the subnet's alpha issuance. Root is paid `min(V, r) x alpha_out`, and alpha validators share the rest of the slot. `r` is recomputed from live state every block and does not depend on the subnet's vector, so lowering the validator share takes the difference from alpha validators, not from root. Owner and miner slots are untouched.
- A set call fails with `ValidatorEmissionTooLowToPreserveRoot` when the new validator share is at or below `r` at that block. The comparison uses `u64` parts, the same unit in which the runtime API reports `root_baseline_share`, so the smallest accepted validator share is exactly `root_baseline_share + 1`. With no root stake any positive validator share passes. If `r` later grows past a subnet's validator share, root's cut is capped at the whole slot and alpha validators receive nothing until the owner raises it.
- There is no per-subnet TAO weight. Consensus stake weighting, the TAO-vs-alpha split inside validator dividends, child/parent stake, and the pool side (TAO in, alpha in, the injection cap) all use the global `TaoWeight`. Changing a subnet's vector changes how its alpha is divided. It does not change how much TAO or alpha enters its pool.
- Lock refund on dissolution: the owner's legacy lock refund deducts the owner alpha actually accrued over the subnet's life (`SubnetOwnerCutAccrued`), priced at the current alpha price. It never uses the owner's current share, so zeroing the owner slot before dissolution does not raise the refund.
- Atlas does not read `SubnetEmissionWeights`. Never state a subnet's live split, or that it still runs the default split, without a chain read.

## Staking

- Staking TAO on a subnet converts TAO to alpha through the AMM pool.
- Unstaking converts alpha back to TAO through the same pool.
- There is slippage on both operations, especially with thin liquidity pools.
- Stakers earn rewards through their chosen validators. Validators receive emissions and distribute to their stakers proportionally.
- Stakers can choose how to receive rewards: "Keep" (receive alpha tokens) or "Swap" (alpha is automatically converted to TAO).
- Spec 448 (2026-08-21) added a per-subnet `TotalAlphaStaked` counter (total alpha staked across all hotkeys on a subnet, backfilled by a paged migration), `move_stake_limit` (move stake with slippage protection), linked limit orders, and an all-hotkeys unstake workflow (`unstake-all`).
- `AlphaBalance::MAX` means the live origin position on every debit call (`move_stake*`, `transfer_stake*`, `swap_stake*`). A quoted amount that is not the sentinel and exceeds the live position still fails. Verified in the spec 470 clone (`pallets/subtensor/src/staking/move_stake.rs`, commit `923fd1fa7`).
- Stake shares are stored only in `AlphaV2` and `TotalHotkeySharesV2`. The legacy `Alpha`, `TotalHotkeyShares` and `AlphaMapLastKey` items are not in the runtime metadata. Do not tell a reader to query `Alpha` for a stake position. The conversion migration (`migrate_alpha_v2_and_unstake_dust_v1`) runs in idle-block batches and makes one dust sweep: a converted legacy position worth 1,000 rao to 0.003 TAO is unstaked to its coldkey, and any position worth under 1,000 rao is deleted with no coldkey payout (its TAO value is burned). A position held by conviction locks or registration collateral, or held by the basket escrow, is kept.
- `StakingHotkeys` (a coldkey's index of hotkeys it stakes to) keeps a hotkey until the pair has NO alpha key left on any subnet, counting zero-valued and below-minimum shares, its basket watermark (`BasketClaimed`) is zero, and none of the coldkey's auto-stake destinations (`AutoStakeDestination`) points at it. A tiny or fractional position therefore still lists its hotkey. Setting an auto-stake destination (`set_coldkey_auto_stake_hotkey`) adds the hotkey to the list and is refused (`TooManyStakingHotkeys`) when that would take the list past 256 entries; so is creating a new hotkey for the coldkey. Do not read a listed hotkey as proof of a meaningful stake. Some lists lost entries before that retention rule existed. A bounded repair (`migrate_restore_staking_hotkeys_v1`) re-adds every hotkey that has an `AlphaV2` row or a nonzero `BasketClaimed` watermark for the coldkey, at most 64 rows per idle block, and starts only after the alpha conversion and the staking-hotkeys cleanup have finished. It moves no stake and changes no balance. Whether it has finished was not read, so a list can still miss a hotkey the coldkey holds stake under. Do not read an unlisted hotkey as proof of no stake: check `AlphaV2` or a stake runtime API.
- Staking-list caps: a coldkey's own staking actions can grow its `StakingHotkeys` list to 256 entries. Other accounts (a `transfer_stake` from another coldkey, a validator's hotkey swap) can grow it only to 128. A coldkey can also refuse ALL list growth caused by other accounts: `set_reject_staking_hotkey_growth` (call index 153) sets a bit in `AccountFlags`. It is off by default and moves no funds. It follows a coldkey swap onto a fresh coldkey; when a swap merges into a coldkey that is already in use or has its own flags, the destination's flags win (see the coldkey swaps section). With it on, an incoming stake transfer must land on a hotkey the receiver already lists or owns; otherwise it fails with `AccountRejectsStakingHotkeyGrowth`. The receiver can still establish a new hotkey through its own staking action. The EVM staking precompile exposes `setRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(bool)` and the view `getRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(bytes32)`, so a contract can check the recipient before a transfer that would be refused. Whether a given coldkey has the flag on needs a chain read.
- Hotkey swaps and delegators: an all-subnets hotkey swap renames every delegator's entry. A single-subnet swap (`swap_hotkey_v2` with a netuid, `keep_stake` false) renames a delegator's entry when its stake under the old hotkey exists nowhere else. When the delegator also holds stake, a basket watermark or an auto-stake target under the old hotkey elsewhere, following needs a second list entry. If the delegator has the opt-out on or is at the 128-entry third-party cap, its stake on that subnet STAYS on the old hotkey, with its auto-stake target and its lock, and the chain emits `HotkeySwapStakeRetained`. The staker can still exit or move that stake. On a root swap, root dividends the retained staker already earned stay claimable under the new hotkey through a claim-only credit (`HotkeySwapBasketCredits`), which does not add a list entry. `claim_root` and coldkey swaps include these credits within their existing bounds, and redeeming one is subject to the claimant's 256-entry cap. The validator's own position is not retained: a single-subnet swap that would push the signing coldkey's list past 256 fails with `TooManyStakingHotkeys` before the swap fee is charged. A rename moves the whole position, so no fractional share is left behind on the old hotkey.
- Childkey delegation fan-in is capped: one child hotkey can have at most 100 parents on a subnet. The cap is checked when a pending child-key change activates, so competing pending updates and automatic parenting cannot get past it. An existing list longer than 100 is not truncated and its rewards do not change; it can shrink or be updated, but it cannot grow.
- An alpha fee payer is refunded like a TAO payer. The alpha sold at withdraw covers the declared fee. TAO the call did not use is re-issued to the payer, not put back by re-staking. A refund too small to open an account for a payer without one rolls back, and the charge stays final. The fee sale is an exit like any other: a fee cannot be paid in a subnet's alpha while that subnet's subtoken is disabled, nor from root stake still inside its unlock interval. Verified in `pallets/transaction-fee/src/lib.rs` in the spec 470 clone (commit `923fd1fa7`).

## Coldkey swaps

- A coldkey swap moves everything the old coldkey holds to the new one: stake positions, basket watermarks and claim-only credits, owned hotkeys, subnet ownership and leases, locks, miner collateral, queued subnet registrations with their escrow, and the free balance. The destination MAY already be in use. Shares on the same `(hotkey, netuid)` add together, basket watermarks add together (an overflow fails the whole swap), and the two staking lists are merged. `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated` is retired and never returned. Do not tell a reader that a coldkey swap needs an unused destination.
- On a merge the destination keeps its own settings: its identity, its `AccountFlags` (including the locked-alpha and staking-list-growth opt-outs) whenever it is in use or has flags set, its decaying-lock choice on a subnet, and any auto-stake destination it set. The source's auto-stake setting carries over only to a blank destination, one with no stake, hotkeys, flags, swap lineage, proxy grants or balance. Coldkey swap lineage is recorded only when the destination has no staking associations and no recorded lineage of its own. Per-coldkey rate-limit clocks and hotkey-swap cooldowns keep the later of the two stamps. Proxy grants, proxy announcements and `RealPaysFee` consents stay with the old coldkey; a swap never moves delegation authority.
- A swap is refused when the destination is a registered hotkey (`NewColdKeyIsHotkey`), or holds any lock with locked alpha or residual conviction (`ActiveLockExists`). It is also refused when the destination is in use or has flags set, rejects locked alpha, and the source has active locks (`AccountRejectsLockedAlpha`). A destination with the staking-list growth opt-out refuses a source that would add hotkeys it does not already list (`AccountRejectsStakingHotkeyGrowth`). Finally, a swap larger than one call admits fails with `ColdkeySwapTooHeavy`: more than 256 hotkeys across both coldkeys' staking lists and credits, 1,024 source stake positions, 128 destination lock rows, 32 queued registrations owned by the source, or a registration queue longer than 256 entries.
- Two announcement calls. `announce_coldkey_swap` (call index 125) commits to a hash of the destination and neither checks nor reserves it. `announce_coldkey_swap_v2` (call index 155; EVM neuron precompile `announceColdkeySwapV2(bytes32)`) takes the destination in clear text and runs the destination checks before charging the fee. It then RESERVES the destination: the reserved account cannot be registered or swapped in as a hotkey, cannot receive stake or locks from another coldkey, and cannot be named by another announcement (`ColdkeySwapDestinationReserved`). The reservation ends when the swap executes, the announcement is cleared or reset, the destination releases it (`release_own_coldkey_swap_destination_reservation`, call index 154), or the announcement's execution block plus `ColdkeySwapReannouncementDelay` passes. Every V2 announcement pays the key-swap cost, reannouncements included. It also records whether the destination was blank when announced, so balance sent to the destination during the delay cannot change whether the source's auto-stake setting carries over.
- `swap_coldkey_announced` keeps the announcement when execution fails, so a refused swap can be retried after the cause is fixed without a new announcement.
- Recovery lock: root's `reset_coldkey_swap_keep_locked` (call index 156) clears the announcement, any reservation and any dispute, but keeps the coldkey restricted. It can announce again, dispute, execute a swap, or send an encrypted `submit_encrypted` transaction. It cannot clear the announcement or make any other call. A completed swap to a different coldkey, or root's full `reset_coldkey_swap` (call index 128), lifts the lock. While a dispute is active, every call from the coldkey is blocked.

## Root Reborn (live; root weights retired at spec 469)

- Root Reborn is how ROOT dividends are handled. It is LIVE on Finney. There is **no weight-vector curation**: spec 469 retired `set_root_weights` and removed the curation switch.
- IN FORCE now:
  - Only hotkeys REGISTERED ON THE ROOT NETWORK earn root dividends. Root alpha that finds no eligible earner is RECYCLED rather than accumulating to unregistered keys. Root registration is burn-based.
  - Root unstakes sit behind a hold interval, which exists to stop just-in-time dividend sniping at epoch boundaries.
  - Root dividends are reinvested into per-validator BETA BASKETS: one fund per validator, held under a global escrow coldkey. Deposits mint fund shares at pre-deposit realizable NAV (valued at executable quotes, not spot marks) so late stakers cannot dilute or skim; stakers accrue claimable shares via a per-validator rate accumulator, and `claim_root` redeems into root TAO. Spec 453 rejects user stake transfers into the basket escrow (`CannotUseSystemAccount`). Spec 454 narrowed `claim_root`: only root-relevant hotkeys are claimed, admission is `root-relevant hotkeys + actual basket rows <= 256`, and a holding that rounds to a zero take leaves the claim unsettled instead of charging a fee for nothing. Do not describe `claim_root` as pro-rata across every holding. Redemption skips dust rows. A fund row is neither sold nor paid when the whole holding is worth less than the row-dust floor, or the claimant's slice is worth less than the slice-dust floor, at the anchored mark. Code defaults, chain storage unset at finalized block 9348505: row floor is min(1 TAO, 10 bps of anchored NAV); slice floor is 0.0001 TAO. A skipped slice stays in the fund. The cash-first claim path is not live.
  - The legacy calls `set_root_claim_type` (122) and `sudo_set_num_root_claims` (123) are RETIRED, along with the auto-claim scheduler. Legacy claim state was drained by a multi-block seed migration, which has COMPLETED (`BetaBasketSeedInProgress` is false).
  - Deposits are queued rather than priced inline during an epoch, which is what fixed the 24-second epoch blocks.
- ROOT WEIGHTS RETIRED (spec 469): the upstream migration `migrate_remove_root_weights` retired the `set_root_weights` extrinsic, cleared every `Weights[ROOT]` vector (none was seeded in its place), and removed the `RootWeightSettingEnabled` curation switch (false since spec 464, block 9088729, 2026-09-17). Root dividends accumulate in place on the subnet they arrive on, and deposits mirror a fund's current holdings. A fund's composition changes ONLY through basket trades (`swap_basket` or `swap_basket_many`). Do not describe validators as curating baskets with weight vectors. Non-root `Weights` rows are untouched.
- BASKET TRADING (ON): `BasketTradingEnabled` is **true** on chain (explicit `0x01` at finalized block 9348505, 2026-10-24). It was set by a one-shot stamped migration (`enable_basket_trading_v1`, commit `cb204a7bb`). The switch gates both basket trade calls, the only way a fund's composition changes. A hotkey can still be frozen individually (`BasketTradingFrozen`). `sudo_set_basket_trading_enabled(false)` remains a durable emergency off: a later upgrade does not flip it back.
- TWO TRADE CALLS: `swap_basket` (call index 150) trades one leg: sell `amount` of the origin holding for TAO, buy the destination. `swap_basket_many` (call index 151) takes 1 to 64 legs (`MAX_BASKET_SWAP_LEGS`), each `(origin_netuid, destination_netuid, amount, min_amount_out)`. It flushes queued dividend deposits once and values the fund once, then applies every `swap_basket` check to each leg in order: gates, ownership, available stake, the trade minimum, the 2% slippage band, the turnover budget, the liquidity cap, the concentration cap, and the caller's `min_amount_out` floor.
- TRADE MINIMUM: a basket trade (each `swap_basket`, each `swap_basket_many` leg) must sell for at least max(`DefaultMinStake`, `BasketMinTradeTao`) of TAO at its sell quote, so a trade cannot cost more in fees (about 0.006 TAO) than it rebalances. `BasketMinTradeTao` is root-settable (`AdminUtils::sudo_set_basket_min_trade_tao`, call index 113, in rao); its code default is 0.5 TAO, and zero removes this extra floor but leaves `DefaultMinStake`. Its live value was not part of the finalized-block 9348505 read: do not quote 0.5 TAO as the live minimum without a chain read. Transaction validation quotes the sell without changing state and rejects a smaller leg before it enters the pool or pays a fee. The check also reaches basket calls nested in Utility (`batch`, `batch_all`, `force_batch`, `as_derivative`, `if_else`) and Proxy (`proxy`, `proxy_announced`) wrappers, judged against the real account. A dispatched trade under the minimum fails with `AmountTooLow`. The legs are atomic: if any leg fails, every leg rolls back, but the dividend flush stays settled, as it does for a failed `swap_basket`. An empty leg list fails with `BasketSwapBatchEmpty`. The `BasketTrading` proxy admits both calls and nothing else. Among the broad proxies, only `Any` admits them.
- CONCENTRATION CAP: `BasketConcentrationCap` is the largest share of a fund's NAV one holding may reach through a basket-trade buy (each `swap_basket` or each `swap_basket_many` leg), u16-normalized (65535 = 100%). It is **7865, about 12% of fund NAV** (explicit at finalized block 9348505, 2026-10-24; governance-set, the code default is 4096 = 1/16), so a traded fund spreads across at least 9 holdings. It checks buys only: a holding that grows past the cap through price or in-place dividends is left alone and can only be sold down. It is not enforced while the chain has fewer subnets than the cap demands. It replaced `RootWeightsCap[ROOT]` at spec 469; a governance-set cap carried over.
- Therefore: root dividends are NOT steered by validator weight vectors. Saying they "accumulate in place" is the live fact, and a validator changes its fund's composition only by trading with `swap_basket` or `swap_basket_many`. Saying validators set root weights is stale.
- `stake_into_basket` (a direct TAO buy-in to one validator's basket, `btcli root allocate`) is OPEN at spec 452: the spec-443 `CallDisabled` gate is gone from the dispatchable (verified at tag v452). It deposits at realizable NAV and credits entitlement immediately; the buyer pays the entry slippage.
- BETA PRICING is defined ON-CHAIN since spec 450 (2026-08-27). Beta is a share count in ONE validator's fund, not a coin and not comparable across validators; raw price is fund value / shares and a new fund starts at 1 TAO per share. Because raw prices carry arbitrary historical baselines, the chain stamps each fund a `BetaBaseline` at its first share mint (raw price / basket index level at that block, both marked at REALIZABLE quotes bounded by pool depth, so a thin-pool spot pump cannot poison it). DISPLAY PRICE = raw price / baseline, so a new fund starts ON the index line; DISPLAY BETA = beta x baseline, so display beta x display price is still the TAO value. The BASKET INDEX (`BetaIndexSnapshot`) is the NAV-weighted average of live funds, flow-neutral by construction, maintained by a paged background sweep. `BasketTwr` is the per-fund total-return accumulator and the canonical staker-yield series. All of it is served by `BetaBasketRuntimeApi` v3; btcli and the SDK pass the chain's numbers through. A beta price or fund return is a LIVE, fund-specific quantity: NEVER quote one from this corpus.
- Spec 451 (2026-08-28) hardened claims: a terminal swap failure in one holding no longer blocks a healthy `claim_root`, and pending basket deposits no longer block root stake changes.

## Proxy call filters and the crowdloan pallet

- Proxy call filters have no coverage in earlier corpus snapshots. Spec 453 closed nested-proxy filter laundering. Spec 454 re-allowed `transfer_stake` through `ContractCallFilter`. Spec 455 (live 2026-09-07, block 9,018,443) denies EVM, Contracts and Crowdloan to the `NonTransfer` and `NonFungible` proxy kinds. Broader proxy roles retain those routes. A restricted coldkey proxy that used to reach those pallets cannot after 455.
- Hotkey-operations proxies: `Validate` (proxy type index 19) and `Weights` (index 20). The real account is the validator HOTKEY, which signs the grant. `Validate` admits every weights call (set, commit, reveal, timelocked, CRv3, per-mechanism and batched), `serve_axon`, `serve_axon_tls`, `serve_prometheus`, `associate_evm_key` and `Commitments.set_commitment`, which is deposit-free on this runtime. `Weights` admits the weights calls only, so a third-party weight setter cannot re-point the axon or the EVM key. Neither admits stake, transfers, registration, key swaps, child keys, take changes, basket calls, or any wrapper (`Utility`, `Proxy`, `Multisig`, `Sudo`), so neither can move or lock the real account's TAO or alpha. `NonTransfer`, `NonFungible` and `NonCritical` still admit the same hotkey calls. Do not say a validator must share its hotkey to let an operator set weights.
- Proxy fee redirection (`RealPaysFee`) follows up to THREE nested `proxy` levels. Every hop needs the real account's opt-in for its delegate, and the deepest consenting real pays, with no tip. A batch of `proxy` calls that all share one real can form the last level; the lookup never descends into batch items. `proxy_announced` does not redirect fees.
- A fee-free wrapper (a `proxy` call or a Utility `batch`, `batch_all`, `force_batch`, `as_derivative` or `if_else` whose dispatched calls are all fee-free, such as weights calls) is checked call by call at transaction validation. Every proxy hop must be a zero-delay grant whose type admits the call, and each inner call must pass the checks it would face as an outer call of its account. A call that fails is rejected before inclusion (a disallowed proxy type gives `InvalidRealAccount`), so it cannot ride into a block for free.
- The crowdloan pallet is present in the runtime. Spec 455's proxy change is the first corpus coverage of it: `NonTransfer` and `NonFungible` proxies cannot dispatch crowdloan calls. Do not invent crowdloan mechanics beyond that filter fact.

## EVM precompiles

- The EVM exposes the final EIP-2537 BLS12-381 precompiles at addresses `0x0b` to `0x11`: G1 add, G1 multi-scalar multiplication, G2 add, G2 multi-scalar multiplication, pairing check, map field element to G1, map field element to G2. Single-point multiplication is the MSM call with one point. They sit alongside the standard Ethereum precompiles and the Bittensor-specific ones. Do not describe BLS12-381 operations as unavailable on the subtensor EVM.
- Stake exits through the EVM staking precompile or the contracts chain extension (`remove_stake*`, `move_stake*`, `transfer_stake*`, `swap_stake*`, `unstake_all`, `unstake_all_alpha`) reserve the `StakingHotkeys` walk for the signer's current list length plus one, never more than the declared cap, plus one read to size it. For `unstake_all*` each envelope slot is sized that way. A coldkey with a short list therefore needs less gas than the call's declared weight. Ordinary signed extrinsics still declare the walk at the cap, because earlier extrinsics in the block can grow the list. The contracts environment cannot refund, so a contract pays the sized charge in full; the EVM path refunds unused weight after dispatch.
- EIP-7702 authorization lists hold at most 255 entries on every entry path: Ethereum transactions, direct EVM calls and creates, and RPC simulations. A longer list is rejected before any signature is recovered (`AuthorizationListTooLarge`). Every entry costs intrinsic gas, including an invalid or duplicate one, and the gas, nonce and balance checks run before the authorization signatures are recovered.
- The runtime-call dispatch precompile (address `0x06`) refuses to dispatch from a static (`STATICCALL`) frame. It and the proxy precompile's `proxyCall` charge gas for every input byte before decoding, and refuse input longer than the normal block length. A call whose weight exceeds the frame's remaining gas fails out of gas and spends only that frame's gas.
- The EVM subnet precompile reads and sets a subnet's liquid-alpha consensus source: `getLiquidAlphaConsensusMode(uint16)` and `setLiquidAlphaConsensusMode(uint16,uint8)`, with 0 = Current, 1 = Previous, 2 = Auto (any other value reverts). The setter dispatches `sudo_set_liquid_alpha_consensus_mode` signed by the caller, so it succeeds only for the subnet owner, inside the admin window and under the owner rate limit.
- An EVM account's transaction nonce survives the reaping of its mapped Substrate balance account. The runtime keeps a retained nonce (`EVM.AccountNonces`, filled lazily from the system nonce) and uses the larger of the two. So a `transferAll(bytes32,bool)` with `keepAlive = false` that reaps the account does not make previously signed EVM transactions valid again, even after the account is funded again.

## Conviction and Locking (recent upgrade)

- Conviction staking lets a coldkey LOCK alpha to a specific hotkey on a subnet, building an on-chain "conviction" score over time. It is a public commitment signal that cannot be silently reversed. (Source: docs.learnbittensor.org/staking-and-delegation/conviction-staking.)
- Locking does NOT change emissions or rewards. Canon, verbatim: "Locking stake does not change the amount of emissions you receive." Locked alpha still earns normal staking rewards. NEVER write that locking or conviction boosts emissions, weight, or rewards.
- Two modes: perpetual (conviction grows toward the locked amount: ~63% at one time constant, ~90% at ~2.3 time constants) and decaying (unwinding forces a public exponential-decay window before the lock is gone, giving advance warning of a large exit).
- The time constant (MaturityRate / UnlockRate) is currently a 90-day half-life and is governance-settable. Do not state a fixed number as permanent.
- Owner specifics: a subnet owner's per-epoch cut is NOT auto-locked by default (opt-in). Any lock targeting the owner's hotkey instantly matures conviction to the full locked amount.
- Conviction-based ownership transfer (ACTIVE since spec 432, live on Finney 2026-07-16; verified in the v452 runtime, checked every epoch): if a subnet is at least ONE YEAR old AND the hotkey with the highest rolled aggregate conviction holds, BY ITSELF, more than 18% of the subnet's ELIGIBLE alpha (`SubnetAlphaOut - SubnetProtocolAlpha - AlphaBurned`, saturating; strict greater-than; no transfer when eligible alpha is zero), ownership (both the owner hotkey AND the owner coldkey) transfers automatically to that hotkey, and the new owner's conviction is matured to its full locked amount. The single-hotkey 18% gate arrived with spec 447 (2026-08-14, PR #3083) and REPLACED the aggregate 10%-of-alpha-out gate, which had let unrelated locks (including the incumbent owner's own) supply a challenger's quorum. Coalitions still work: backers lock to the challenger's hotkey, so their conviction lands in that hotkey's aggregate. So ownership can change by means other than deregistration. The one-year age gate means only subnets registered over a year ago are eligible; treat any claim that a SPECIFIC subnet changed hands this way as needing chain evidence.
- Constraints: staked alpha cannot be unstaked below the locked amount; locked alpha cannot move cross-subnet directly; conviction is wiped if the subnet is deregistered.
- Locked-alpha transfer flag (added with the June 2026 upgrade, subtensor v3.4.6-421): coldkeys REJECT incoming locked alpha by default. A coldkey must opt in (via set_reject_locked_alpha set to disabled) to receive locked alpha through stake transfers or coldkey swaps. One exception: a coldkey swap onto a fresh coldkey (no staking associations and no flags set) moves the owner's own locks regardless. A destination that is in use or has flags and still rejects locked alpha refuses a swap from a source with active locks. This is a transfer-acceptance control only; it does not change emissions or rewards.

## Chain Buys vs Emission Share (CRITICAL DISTINCTION)

- **emission_pct / emission_percent**: The subnet's share of total network emissions. This is how much of the 3,600 daily TAO the subnet receives. Driven by the gated price-based formula (EMA price x (1 - miner_burn), normalized, then passed through the emission gate).
- **emission_buy_pct / emission_chain_buys_percent**: The percentage of a subnet's emissions that are being market-bought through the AMM (chain buys). This is a SEPARATE metric.
- **emission_miner_burn / miner_burn**: A THIRD, distinct metric. The proportion (0..1) of a subnet's miner emission withheld from miners (sent to the owner/burn key) in a tempo. It is NOT emission share and NOT chain buys. Under the price-based model it directly cuts emission share via the (1 - miner_burn) term: a higher miner_burn lowers the subnet's network emission share. Keep all three terms distinct.
- These metrics are independent. A subnet can have 5% emission share and 0% chain buys. This is normal and can be positive.
- The per-block alpha injection cap is now root_prop-based (root_prop x alpha_emission). As a subnet matures (alpha issuance grows, root_prop falls), more of its TAO emission can no longer be injected as pool liquidity and is routed to chain buys instead. Rising chain buys on an older subnet is therefore a structural feature of maturity, not a warning sign.
- Low or zero chain buys is NOT a risk. It can indicate the subnet is healthy enough that organic demand covers growth without needing emission-funded buying pressure.
- When writing about chain buys, ALWAYS use the term "chain buys" or "chain buy rate." NEVER call it "emission buy" or "emission rate" as this will be confused with the subnet's emission share.
- The "emission acceleration ratio" (chain_buys / ema) is a momentum signal, not a health metric. A ratio below 1.0 or at 0 does not mean the subnet is unhealthy.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- "Emissions are only paid in alpha" --> INCOMPLETE. Both TAO and alpha are injected into the pool each block; at tempo, alpha is distributed to participants by the subnet's emission weight vector, and the injected TAO backs the pool reserve. Stakers can keep alpha or swap to TAO.
- "Every subnet splits emission 18% owner / 41% miners / 41% validators / the split is fixed by the protocol" --> OUTDATED as of spec 481. 18/41/41 is only the default. Each subnet's owner (or root) sets its `[burn, recycle, owner, validator, miner]` vector within bounds (owner at most 33%, validator at most 50%, miner at least 33%). Read `SubnetEmissionWeights` before quoting a subnet's split.
- "A subnet owner can cut root stakers' dividends by lowering the validator share" --> WRONG. Root is paid its baseline share off the top of the validator slot every block, computed with the default validator share and the global TaoWeight. Lowering the validator share takes the difference from alpha validators. A change that would leave alpha validators nothing at that block is rejected.
- "Owner cut is controlled by `SubnetOwnerCut` / `OwnerCutEnabled`" --> OUTDATED as of spec 481. Those setters fail with `Deprecated`, and no emission path reads the items. The owner's share is the owner slot of the subnet's emission weight vector; 0 disables it.
- "Each subnet has its own TAO weight / a lower validator share raises the subnet's TAO weight" --> WRONG. There is one global `TaoWeight`. A per-subnet TAO-weight multiplier was written during development and dropped before spec 481 shipped.
- "Subnet emissions are based on net TAO flows / Taoflow" --> OUTDATED as of June 2026. Emissions reverted to a PRICE-based model. Taoflow (flow-based) was the model only from November 2025 to June 2026. Saying emissions are price-based is now CORRECT, not an error.
- "Emission share is root_prop x EMA price x (1 - miner_burn)" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-07-16 (spec 432). root_prop was removed from the share. The current share is EMA price x (1 - miner_burn), normalized, then passed through the emission gate (spec 440, 2026-07-27). root_prop still caps alpha injection and splits root dividends; it just no longer weights the share.
- "Every subnet with nonzero demand earns a proportional emission share" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-07-27 (spec 440). The emission gate collapses below-bar subnets' emission toward zero and redistributes it to above-bar subnets. Above-bar subnets earn MORE than their raw demand share. Independently, a subnet with the pool-side emission switch off earns no TAO even with substantial demand share (see the pool-side emission switch section).
- "This subnet has demand but earns no TAO, so it is below the emission-gate bar" --> NOT NECESSARILY. The bar is a continuous function of demand share. The pool-side emission switch is a binary root knob. A subnet above the bar with the switch off still earns no TAO; alpha distribution continues. Check `SubnetEmissionEnabled` before attributing a zero to the bar.
- "`claim_root` redeems pro-rata across every holding" --> OUTDATED as of spec 454 (2026-09-04). Only root-relevant hotkeys are claimed; admission is `root-relevant hotkeys + actual basket rows <= 256`; a holding that rounds to a zero take leaves the claim unsettled.
- "The emission gate bar is the q-mass crossing share / subnets above the bar carry q of demand" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-08-03 (spec 441). The bar is now RANK-PINNED: EmissionBarRank (N) pins theta to the Nth-largest positive demand share, and EmissionBarQuantile is inert while N > 0. Q-mass is only the N = 0 fallback. Code default at v443 is N = 32 (the release notes say 64 and are wrong).
- "A subnet crossed the bar, so its demand changed" --> NOT NECESSARILY. A rank-pinned bar is itself a demand share and moves as the distribution moves, so a subnet can change side with a stationary share. Check the bar's previous value before attributing a crossing to the subnet.
- "Root Reborn curation is live / validators steer root dividends with `set_root_weights` / `RootWeightSettingEnabled` is true" --> OUTDATED. The switch went false at spec 464 (2026-09-17, block 9088729), and spec 469 retired `set_root_weights`, cleared `Weights[ROOT]`, and removed the switch. Dividends accumulate in place. Do not attribute basket allocation to a validator's weight vector.
- "The root basket cap is `RootWeightsCap` on a weight vector" --> OUTDATED as of spec 469. The cap is `BasketConcentrationCap` (7865, about 12% of fund NAV, explicit at finalized block 9348505) and guards basket-trade buys only (`swap_basket` and each `swap_basket_many` leg). `BasketTradingEnabled` gates both calls and is explicit true.
- "A basket rebalance takes one `swap_basket` call per leg / a multi-leg rebalance can half-complete" --> WRONG. `swap_basket_many` runs up to 64 legs in one call, and they are atomic: one failed leg rolls back every leg. Each leg still passes every `swap_basket` check, including the trade minimum.
- "Any basket trade above the staking minimum is accepted / a dust basket trade only costs its fee" --> WRONG. A basket leg worth under max(`DefaultMinStake`, `BasketMinTradeTao`) at its sell quote is rejected at transaction validation, before inclusion, even inside a Utility batch or Proxy call. `BasketMinTradeTao` is root-settable (code default 0.5 TAO); read it before quoting a number.
- "Every subnet runs Yuma Consensus / every subnet has up to 64 validator permits" --> WRONG. A subnet owner can select Null consensus: one permit for the largest-stake hotkey, miner rewards from its weights alone, dividends pro rata to stake. Which subnets run Null needs a chain read.
- "PoW registration is gone / registration always burns TAO" --> WRONG. `pow_register` is fee-free and burns no TAO on any subnet whose owner enabled it. It is off by default, so check the subnet before saying it is available.
- "Only root can set a subnet's PoW difficulty / `min_difficulty`" --> WRONG. The subnet owner or root sets `min_difficulty` and `difficulty` (and `max_difficulty`) on a non-root subnet, under the admin window and a per-parameter owner rate limit. Only netuid 0 stays root-only.
- "A basket holding can reach at most 1/16 of fund NAV" --> STALE. The live `BasketConcentrationCap` is 7865 (about 12%, explicit at finalized block 9348505); 4096 = 1/16 is only the code default. Read it before quoting a cap.
- "A hotkey swap moves every delegator's stake to the new hotkey" --> NOT ALWAYS. An all-subnets swap does. A single-subnet swap leaves a delegator's stake on the old hotkey when following would add a staking-list entry the delegator refuses (growth opt-out) or cannot take (128-entry third-party cap); the chain emits `HotkeySwapStakeRetained`. Stake left on the old hotkey is not a failed swap.
- "A coldkey swap needs an unused destination / fails with `ColdKeyAlreadyAssociated`" --> OUTDATED as of spec 483. A swap can merge into an already-used coldkey: shares and basket watermarks add together, staking lists merge, and the destination's own flags, identity and auto-stake settings win. A destination that is a hotkey, holds an active lock, or has an opt-out the source would breach still refuses.
- "A new subnet earns no TAO until root turns its emission switch on" --> OUTDATED as of spec 483. Registration writes `SubnetEmissionEnabled` on; the owner's `start_call` is the remaining step. Root can still switch a subnet off, so read the switch before naming an off subnet.
- "To let an operator set weights, a validator must share its hotkey or grant a broad proxy" --> WRONG. The hotkey can grant a `Weights` proxy (weights calls only) or a `Validate` proxy (weights, serving, EVM-key association, commitments). Neither can move stake or TAO.
- "Anyone can transfer stake to any coldkey" --> NOT ALWAYS. A receiver with the staking-list growth opt-out (`set_reject_staking_hotkey_growth`) accepts transfers only onto hotkeys it already lists or owns, and every receiver's list can grow through other accounts only to 128 entries.
- Cash-first root claims are not live. That path was written for spec 468 and dropped before the spec shipped. A claim skips dust rows. It does not pay from a cash slot.
- "The subnet takeover gate is 10% of total conviction" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-08-14 (spec 447). The gate is ONE hotkey's own rolled conviction above 18% of eligible alpha; other keys' locks do not add to it.
- "Conviction ownership transfer is proposed / not yet active" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-07-16 (spec 432). The enforcement is live and checked every epoch (one-year age gate + the single-hotkey 18% gate since spec 447).
- "A beta token has a price like a coin" --> WRONG framing. Beta is a share count in one validator's fund; its TAO value is beta x (fund value / all beta), and since spec 450 the chain publishes an index-normalized DISPLAY price per fund. Beta counts of different validators do not add. Never quote a beta price or fund return from this corpus.
- "Validators mine" --> WRONG. Validators evaluate and set weights. Miners produce work.
- "TAO is burned when staking" --> WRONG. TAO goes into the AMM pool, it is not destroyed. TAO IS burned when registering on a subnet (different operation).
- "All subnets do AI" --> WRONG. Some do DeFi, gaming, data, infrastructure, etc.
- "Stakers earn directly from the network" --> MISLEADING. Stakers earn through validators.
- "New subnets are immune for 4 months / 120 days" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-10-14. Immunity is 60 days (432,000 blocks); root lowered it with no runtime upgrade.
- "The subnet lock cost halves every 16 days" --> OUTDATED as of 2026-10-14. The lock reduction interval is 8 days (57,600 blocks).
- "Daily emissions are 7,200 TAO" --> OUTDATED. Current daily emission is ~3,600 TAO (0.5 TAO/block) after the first halving. Halvings are supply-triggered, not calendar-based.
- "0% emission buy rate" as a risk --> WRONG. Chain buys at 0% is not a risk indicator. Never flag it as one.
- "Emission buy" without specifying "chain buys" --> CONFUSING. Always say "chain buy rate" not "emission buy" to avoid confusion with the subnet's emission share.
